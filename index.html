Computer vision and IoT

AI-based smart bin

A waste segregation system that classifies items with a CNN and a YOLOv8 object-detection model running on a Raspberry Pi, then drives servo and stepper motors to sort them. Ultrasonic and IR sensors feed the system, and Telegram sends alerts. Built to make recycling more efficient.

  • YOLOv8
  • CNN
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Ultrasonic and IR sensors
  • Telegram

Full-stack web and AI

Resume documenter

A Django web app where users sign up, upload PDF resumes against job openings and manage them from user and admin dashboards. PyMuPDF and the Google Gemini API pull out name, contact details, education, skills and experience, then generate AI summaries, job categories and ranking analysis. It also covers job-role management and live resume search.
